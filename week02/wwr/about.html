Banith Safaris Africa

Banith Safaris A frica Ltd is a tour and travel company offering gorilla trakking, wildlife game drives, hikes to the top of Murchison Falls, Nile River excursions, and multi-day regional road trips planned around travelers' specific interests and abilities. Started its operations in 2014 and located in central business district of Kampala Uganda.

History

The company started in 2014 and officially registered in 2016. Started its official adventures in 2016 with its first group of 6 clients from Netherlands. Banith Safaris Africa is a travel company specializing in creating fun adventure packages for all visitors of all ages
